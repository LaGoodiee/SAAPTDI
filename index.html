About SAAPTDI

Security and Peace Awareness Through Dialogue Initiative

Promoting peace, security, dialogue and responsible citizenship. Launched on 25 March 2026 as an initiative of Al-Bayan Interfaith Centre.

Vision

Communities that are safe, peaceful and harmonious, where security and dialogue work together.

Mission

To raise security awareness, build peace through dialogue, and equip professionals and young leaders with practical skills and knowledge.

Core values

Dialogue

Conversation across faiths and communities before conflict.

Integrity

Honest, accountable work with partners and participants.

Responsible citizenship

Every person has a part in keeping their community safe.

Excellence

Training and research that meet international professional standards.

Objectives and focus areas

  • Spread security awareness and intelligence-led education.
  • Prevent conflict through interreligious dialogue and mediation.
  • Build professional capacity through certified international training.
  • Develop young leaders and an international ambassador network.
  • Form partnerships with institutions that share our goals.

Relationship with ABIC

Al-Bayan Interfaith Centre (RC 125222) is the parent organisation. SAAPTDI has its own identity and programmes and operates under ABIC's guidance and registration.

Certification and professional development

Professional Training Certificate in Digital Forensics and Cybercrime Investigation

A multi-session international programme. Complete it to receive a SAAPTDI professional training certificate.

Who should attend

  • Law enforcement officers and investigators
  • Cybersecurity and IT professionals
  • Intelligence analysts and security professionals
  • Legal practitioners
  • Peacebuilders, community and religious leaders, NGOs
  • Students and aspiring experts

What you gain

  • Practical techniques for collecting, analysing and preserving digital evidence
  • Insight into real-world case studies and investigative workflows
  • Skills to turn information into actionable intelligence
  • A professional training certificate on completion

Register in four steps

  1. Choose sessionsSee the calendar on the events page.
  2. Pay the feeIndividual registration is ₦5,000. ABIC core members attend free.
  3. Send your detailsMessage us on WhatsApp or email with proof of payment.
  4. Receive joining detailsLinks are shared with registered participants.
